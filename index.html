I'm currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Data Science at Universität Potsdam while working as a Working Student at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development. There, I support an ongoing research project with a focus on data analysis — picking up Julia and learning Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) alongside my coursework.
